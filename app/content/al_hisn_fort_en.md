# Al Hisn Fort

Imagine walking through Sharjah more than 200 years ago.

The city is quieter. Traditional homes surround you, the sea is close by, and standing at the heart of it all is a great fort — Al Hisn.

Built in the early 19th century, Al Hisn was more than just a building. It was a home, a place of leadership, and a shield protecting the people of Sharjah.

As you step closer, look at its thick walls and tall towers.

These walls have seen generations come and go. They have witnessed important decisions, family gatherings, and the changing story of Sharjah.

For a moment, imagine the sound of footsteps inside the fort, the voices of families in the courtyard, and the guards watching over the city from the towers.

But Al Hisn is not only a place to remember the past.

Today, it stands among the modern streets of Sharjah, quietly reminding us of where the city began.

So, as you walk through its doors, don’t think of yourself as simply visiting an old fort.

**You are stepping into a story.**

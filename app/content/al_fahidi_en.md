# Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood

Step off the modern street and the city changes around you. Narrow lanes replace highways, coral-and-gypsum walls replace glass towers, and above every rooftop rises a tall, four-sided tower open to the sky.

Those towers are barjeel — wind towers. Long before air conditioning, they were Dubai's answer to the desert heat, built to catch even the faintest breeze and funnel it down into the rooms below.

This neighbourhood, once known as Bastakiya, was home to merchant families who settled along the Dubai Creek, trading pearls, textiles, and spices between the Gulf and the wider world.

Wander its lanes today and you'll find the old courtyard houses reborn as galleries, cafes, and cultural centres, still shaded by the very wind towers that once cooled the merchants who built them.

**The breeze you feel here has been finding its way through these lanes for well over a century.**
